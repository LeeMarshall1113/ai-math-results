Title: The geometric thickness of K14 is 4
Upload type: Publication / Preprint
Creators: Marshall, Lee
Description (abstract): LEE writes. Technical summary for reference: no straight-line drawing of the complete graph
  K14 has its edges split into three crossing-free layers, so its geometric thickness is 4 (the upper bound is
  Dillencourt–Eppstein–Hirschberg 2000, Thm 2.1). The lower bound is a SAT computation over signotopes with a
  hull-size case split and an Euler bound; two independently written encoders give UNSAT, and every leaf LRAT proof
  was checked by cake_lpr.
Keywords: geometric thickness; complete graph; straight-line drawing; graph drawing; signotope; SAT; LRAT;
  computer-assisted proof
