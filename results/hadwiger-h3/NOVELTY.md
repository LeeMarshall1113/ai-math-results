# Novelty search (2026-10-24, 2026-10-25, 2026-10-31)

Question: is there an earlier claim of H₃ ≤ 13, or any general bound below 14 for Hadwiger's covering problem in
ℝ³? Is Lemma M (long chord from the minimal parallelotope) known?

## arXiv (export API, newest first)

Queries: illumination in math.MG; Hadwiger AND covering; homothetic AND (covering OR copies); the arXiv author
page of A. Prymak (2025 onward).

Relevant hits, none claiming a bound below 14 for general bodies in ℝ³:
* 2609.23913, fractional illumination, asymptotic rate.
* 2607.08944, illumination of primitive polytopes.
* 2510.25968 and 2507.08712 (Arman, Kaire, Prymak), cap bodies; 2507.08712 restates 14 as the best general bound.
* 2501.10570, 2410.12021, 2408.01341, 2407.11331 (Sun–Vritsiou, 1-unconditional bodies), 2407.10314: special
  classes or other settings.
* 2404.00547 (Arman, Bondarenko, Prymak), small dimensions; cites 14 for n = 3.
* 2610.39078 (Arman, Bondarenko, Prymak, Radchenko, 29 Oct 2026), Borsuk number in dimension four, with an
  exact-box computer proof. It does not address Hadwiger covering.
* Other recent Prymak papers (2512.04023, 2606.01376, 2606.30960, 2607.22032): unrelated.

## Zenodo (REST API, most recent first)

Queries: "Hadwiger covering", "Hadwiger covering number", "illumination conjecture", "homothets", "smaller
homothetic copies", "Hadwiger Boltyanski illumination". Quoted phrases returned nothing; unquoted queries returned
only unrelated records. A scan of all titles of one prolific account posting certified bound improvements
(126 records, 27 Sep – 30 Oct 2026) found no Hadwiger or illumination record.

## GitHub

* `gh search repos` for "Hadwiger covering", "illumination conjecture", "homothetic covering", "Hadwiger
  illumination", "H_3 <= 13": one scaffold repository for the problem with no result; nothing else relevant.
* A closed, unmerged pull request (June 2026) in a community optimisation-problems repository describes an
  agent-driven attempt at 14 → 13; its own summary states that 13 was not reached.
* google-deepmind/formal-conjectures (issue updated 21 Oct 2026) and teorth/optimizationproblems
  (constant 39a) list 14 as the best upper bound.
* Problem catalogues (AIM problem list and others) list the problem as open with bound 14.

## OSF, openai/math, web

* No relevant OSF preprints.
* openai/math CONTENTS.md: only graph-colouring uses of "Hadwiger".
* Web searches (2026-10-24, -25, -31): all sources cite 14 (Prymak) and 16 (Papadoperakis) as the latest bounds.

## Lemma M

Searched: "minimal volume parallelepiped" with chord/diagonal/width; "illumination 3-dimensional 13"; "covered by
13 smaller homothetic". We found no use of chord or width consequences of the minimal box beyond Papadoperakis's
Lemma 3(a). Lemma M is elementary and may be known to specialists.

## Limits

* MathSciNet, zbMATH full text and non-English sources were not searched.
