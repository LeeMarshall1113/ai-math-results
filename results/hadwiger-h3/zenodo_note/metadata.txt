Title: Every convex body in R^3 can be covered by 13 smaller homothetic copies
Upload type: Publication / Preprint
Creators: Marshall, Lee
Description (abstract): LEE writes. Technical summary for reference: every convex body in R^3 is covered by 13
  translates of its interior, so H_3 <= 13 (previous bound 14, Prymak 2023). Prymak's framework plus a long-chord
  lemma from the minimal circumscribed parallelotope; exact LP certificate with 7,561,583 leaves checked by two
  separately written exact checkers; reduction formalised in Lean 4. Corrects two points in the H_3 <= 14
  argument (symmetry domain; Lemma 2.4(ii) at box corners).
Keywords: Hadwiger covering problem; illumination conjecture; convex body; homothetic copies; minimal
  parallelotope; linear programming; computer-assisted proof; Lean
