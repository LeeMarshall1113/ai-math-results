Title: No torus with ten convex quadrilateral faces
Upload type: Publication / Preprint
Creators: Marshall, Lee
Description (abstract): LEE writes. Technical summary for reference: no acoptic polyhedral torus in R^3 has exactly
  10 strictly convex quadrilateral faces (the F = 10 case of Grünbaum–Szilassi 2009, Conjecture 3.2). The proof
  reduces to the map {4,4}_(3,1) and certifies with an LRAT proof (checked by cake_lpr) that no planar,
  strictly convex realisation exists, even with self-intersections.
Keywords: polyhedral torus; quadrangulation; convex faces; Grünbaum–Szilassi conjecture; acoptic polyhedra;
  equivelar map; oriented matroid; chirotope; SAT; LRAT; computer-assisted proof
