## 3. Exactness of the double lattice (`src/dl_exact.py`, `runs/dl_exact.log`)

**What it proves: SOUND.**
- The reduction is valid. {s^2 + c^2 - 1, 8c^3 - 4c^2 - 4c + 1} has coprime leading monomials
  s^2 and c^3 in lex order (s > c), so it is a Groebner basis. Remainder 0 therefore means ideal
  membership, which means the expression vanishes at (cos pi/7, sin pi/7). The denominators are
  checked nonzero to 50 digits, and the negative controls fail as they should.
- 8c^3 - 4c^2 - 4c + 1 is the minimal polynomial of cos(pi/7).
- cos(n pi/7) = T_n(c) and sin(n pi/7) = s U_{n-1}(c) are exact.
- The frame is the one the chart code uses (`local_lemma_arb.py`: C0 = ((0,0), (1 + u, 0), 2 p2),
  T0 = (0, 0, pi/7)).
- So the following are proved:
  - g(0) = 0 for all 5 contacts;
  - area(s2) = A/(2 delta_DL) = acrit;
  - the translation edge is (1 + c, 0, 0).
- f = 0 on the three DL edges holds by construction. The two edges to H2p2 have b - a = +/- pi/7
  exactly, so they are R-fixed; the translation edge has a = b = 0, so it is S-fixed. This holds
  for any coefficients, including mu*. So b(0) = acrit exactly.
- I confirmed delta_DL = (2/97)(-111 + 492u - 356u^2) = 0.89269... against Kallus,
  arXiv:1305.0289, and the double-lattice optimality against Kuperberg and Kuperberg, DCG 5
  (1990).

**The null-vector argument: SOUND, and the symbolic G v = 0 is not needed.**
- `local_radius_arb.setup` gets V from an arb solve of the 5x5 system N v = -G0[:, 1], where G0
  encloses the exact DG(0).
- Since the solve succeeded, every matrix in the ball, including the exact one, is nonsingular.
  So rank DG(0) = 5, and the exact kernel vector n (y1 = 1) lies in V.
- Gamma'(0) = DPhi(0)^{-1} e_6 is the kernel vector normalised by the 6th coordinate. With the
  6th coordinate read as n.z, which is legitimate because n is in V and V is the same ball in every
  chart, Gamma'(0) = n/|n|^2.
- So beta'(0) = 0 iff grad b(0) . n = 0. That is the definition of mu*.
- The slope `di` of `f_arb.exactify` excludes 0, so mu* is the unique root, and mu* is in MU,
  which is inside HEPT_MU.
- Charts and B&B both certify for every mu in their intervals, so for the one f with mu*.
- One caveat: the exported v' = fl(n), not n (FLAW-4).

## 6. What a Lean formalisation must cover

1. **Definitions.** Regular heptagon H(c, t) and packing; upper density (choose one); acrit, tau,
   RTH, rb0.
2. **L1 / L1'.** Zorn saturation; 2u-separation; every open disc of radius 2 meets C.
3. **Finite Delaunay.** Existence, for a finite non-collinear set, of a triangulation of the convex
   hull whose triangles have empty open circumdiscs. Mathlib has none; prove it via the lower
   convex hull of the paraboloid lift, or by termination of edge flips. Then Lemmas 2.1, 2.2 (the
   inscribed angle theorem) and Corollary 2.3.
4. **Bookkeeping and averaging.** Lemmas 3.1-3.4; L5 steps 1-5, including the angle-sum count, the
   strip and degree packing bounds, and the limit.
5. **The transfer f and the table D5.**
   - f as an explicit trigonometric polynomial with the 100 float coefficients, with coefficient 9
     shifted by mu*;
   - Lemmas 0.1 and 0.2, and the bound |f| < 0.2436;
   - D5 as the bilinear interpolant on the given 30 x 21 float nodes and grid;
   - Dhat and Chat.
6. **Reduction.** Canonical reduction Lemma 1.1 and the domain bounds. Lemma 1.2 is optional.
7. **Statements (M) and (O).** Their certification needs a checkable certificate, which does not
   exist today: per-box decisions are not logged. Required pieces:
   - the outward-rounding interval layer (`rig.py` contract: dn/up, own sin/cos/atan/sqrt
     enclosures, Taylor coefficients);
   - forward AD of orders 1 and 2, and the mean-value and Taylor-2 lower bounds;
   - the SAT completeness lemma for convex polygons;
   - the forced-axis dual lemma and the vertex-in-slab lemma;
   - the bilinear corner bound with its 1e-12 pad, and the pad covering the round-to-nearest
     arithmetic in `obtuse_extra`;
   - the split tree covering the 45 + 54 slice regions, including the dump, shard and checkpoint
     chains.
8. **Double lattice exactness (H-DL).**
   - Identities in Q(cos pi/7, sin pi/7): ideal membership modulo the minimal polynomial gives the
     5 contacts, the area identity and the translation edge;
   - f = 0 on R- and S-fixed edges;
   - rank 5 and the kernel vector n, from an enclosed 5x5 solve or exactly;
   - mu* as the root of an affine equation.
9. **Tube.**
   - Quantitative Banach / inverse function theorem on a cube;
   - Picard-Lindelöf uniqueness (in Mathlib), the gluing induction, convexity of beta, and the
     monotone path using w > 0;
   - the chart certificates (A)-(D), (E), (G), (Ov) and s* in I_c, rechecked in verified
     rational interval arithmetic on the **inflated** cube K'_c (FLAW-4);
   - the canonical-box-to-z map of `chart_excl.to_z`, with rotation KROT and the cyclic
     relabelling.
10. **Interface.** Statements (M) and (O) exactly as in PROOFS-DRAFT section 1, with the chart
    part at acrit rather than tau.
